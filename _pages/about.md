**PhD student, researcher and product designer** based in Recife, Brazil,
with 9 years of experience as a designer — the last five working on research,
implementing interfaces, building experiments, and performing user analysis
on a diverse range of projects on **Extended Realities (XR)**,
**Computer Vision** and **Data Visualization**.

Currently, I am a Researcher at **ISI-TICS / Senai-PE**, developing extended
reality projects with internal (within SENAI's Network) and external partners,
and a PhD student in Computer Science at **UFPE**. Previously, I worked as
Researcher/Designer at **Voxar Labs** (UFPE) and as Product Designer at
**Pocketfisio**.
